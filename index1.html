About Us

Building a Brighter Future for Malawi Since 1999

Founded by visionary students from the University of Malawi, CAPS is a dynamic non-profit organization committed to eradicating poverty and tackling social challenges across Malawi.

We partner with government agencies, civil society, and local communities

Focus on health, education, agriculture, environment, and human rights

Transforming lives for over two decades through grassroots-driven initiatives

Our Vision

A vibrant Malawi where every individual and community flourishes, free from the grips of poverty, inequality, and social injustice, achieving sustainable prosperity for all.

Our Mission

To empower marginalized Malawians by advancing access to education, health services, sustainable livelihoods, and human rights through collaborative projects and community-led efforts that build equity and resilience.

Our Values

Guiding Principles

The foundation of everything we do at CAPS Malawi

Integrity & Ethics

Committing to transparency and honesty in all our operations and partnerships.

Inclusion

Ensuring every voice is heard, regardless of background, ability, or identity.

Community Empowerment

Equipping individuals with tools for self-reliance and sustainable growth.

Transparency

Holding ourselves and partners to the highest standards for impactful results.

Sustainability

Prioritizing long-term solutions that protect people and the planet.

Compassion

Serving with empathy and understanding for the communities we work with.

Our Work

What We Do

Comprehensive programs addressing root causes of poverty and inequality across key sectors

Health & Wellness Programs

We conduct HIV/AIDS awareness campaigns, distribute preventive tools like condoms, and promote nutrition, clean water, and sanitation in vulnerable areas. Our health initiatives are designed to reduce stigma and improve access to essential services.

HIV/AIDS prevention and awareness
Nutrition and clean water initiatives
Maternal and child health programs

Education & Skills Development

Supporting orphans, vulnerable children, women, and persons with disabilities through access to quality education, Early Childhood Development centers, and vocational training programs that build brighter futures.

Early Childhood Development centers
Vocational and entrepreneurship training
School support for OVCs

Agriculture & Environment

We advance sustainable farming, climate-resilient livelihoods, environmental protection, reforestation, and land rehabilitation, helping communities combat deforestation and climate-related disasters.

Sustainable farming practices
Reforestation and land rehabilitation
Water conservation initiatives

Human Rights & Social Empowerment

Advocating for marginalized groups, we offer counseling for abuse victims, promote gender equality, and fight gender-based violence, fostering inclusive societies through rights-based approaches.

Gender equality advocacy
Protection for vulnerable groups
Counseling and support services
