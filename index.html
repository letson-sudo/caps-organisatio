About Us

Building a Brighter Future for Malawi Since 1999

Founded by visionary students from the University of Malawi, CAPS is a dynamic non-profit organization committed to eradicating poverty and tackling social challenges across Malawi.

We partner with government agencies, civil society, and local communities

Focus on health, education, agriculture, environment, and human rights

Transforming lives for over two decades through grassroots-driven initiatives

Join Our Mission

Our Vision

A vibrant Malawi where every individual and community flourishes, free from the grips of poverty, inequality, and social injustice, achieving sustainable prosperity for all.

Our Mission

To empower marginalized Malawians by advancing access to education, health services, sustainable livelihoods, and human rights through collaborative projects and community-led efforts that build equity and resilience.

Our Values

Guiding Principles

The foundation of everything we do at CAPS Malawi

Integrity & Ethics

Committing to transparency and honesty in all our operations and partnerships.

Inclusion

Ensuring every voice is heard, regardless of background, ability, or identity.

Community Empowerment

Equipping individuals with tools for self-reliance and sustainable growth.

Transparency

Holding ourselves and partners to the highest standards for impactful results.

Sustainability

Prioritizing long-term solutions that protect people and the planet.

Compassion

Serving with empathy and understanding for the communities we work with.
